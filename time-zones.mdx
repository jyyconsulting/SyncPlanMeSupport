---
title: "Time Zones"
---

[SyncPlan.Me](http://SyncPlan.Me) uses the time zone you set in the app settings to calculate event times.

If your personal calendar is in a different time zone, this may cause issues with the calculated work day and event time matching.

<Tip>
  [Click here](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/37064) for instructions on checking or changing your Google calendar time zone.
</Tip>
