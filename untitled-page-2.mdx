<Info>
  We're currently in Private Beta. [Join the waitlist](https://syncplan.me/waitlist) to reserve your spot for the Public Beta.
</Info>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Follow the link in your Invitation Email">
    This will take you to the onboarding pages, which walks you through every step.

    <Tip>
      Login links are only valid for an hour. If the link in your Invitation Email expired, you can request a new one at [https://syncplan.me/login](https://syncplan.me/login)
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your personal Google calendar">
    [SyncPlan.Me](http://SyncPlan.Me) reads your personal calendar and adds events that fall inside your work day to your work calendar.

    <Info>
      We currently only support Google calendar connections. Outlook and other personal calendars are planned for the future.
    </Info>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your work email">
    Although we don't connect directly to your work email or calendar, we need to know where to send the event invites.

    We'll send your work email to confirm that it's your email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your usual work schedule">
    We'll use this to figure out which events on your personal calendar need to get sent to your work calendar.
  </Step>

  </Step>
</Steps>
