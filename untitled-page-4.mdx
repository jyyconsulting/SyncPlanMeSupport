---
title: "Nothing is happening!"
---

Sync runs happen every hour, so if you've just added an event to your personal calendar, you may need to wait for the next run before anything is sent to your work email.

The [**Account Status**](https://syncplan.me/settings/status) page gives you a view of the common reasons why a sync might be blocked:

1. **Work email not verified.** If you recently setup your account, be sure you've verified your work email. Until that's done, we won't send anything there.
2. **Personal calendar not connected.** If your personal calendar gets disconnected, you'll need to reconnect it before we can read your calendar entries.
3. **Sync is turned off.** If you turned syncing off, you'll need to turn it back on before any invites go out.
4. **Preview Mode is turned on.** You'll need to turn Preview Mode off before any invites go out.

Some other common reasons you might not be getting any invites to your work calendar:

- You don't have any personal events that fall in your work day and sync window. Check your personal calendar or the calendar and sync settings to make sure everything is as expected.
