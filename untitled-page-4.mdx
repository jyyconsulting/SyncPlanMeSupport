---
title: "Nothing is happening!"
---

Sync runs happen every hour, so if you've just added an event to your personal calendar, you may need to wait for the next run before anything is sent to your work email.

The [**Account Status**](https://syncplan.me/settings/status) page gives you a view of the common reasons why a sync might be blocked:

1. **Work email not verified.** If you recently setup your account, be sure you've verified your work email. Until that's done, we won't send anything there.
