---
title: "How do I change my account email?"
---

You can change the email address you use to log into your account in [Account Settings](https://syncplan.me/settings/account).

<Info>
  The email for your [SyncPlan.Me](http://SyncPlan.Me) account is separate from your Connected Calendar. You can use your work email for your [SyncPlan.Me](http://SyncPlan.Me) account, but the work email and login email aren't connected in any way.
</Info>

When you request the email change, you'll need to confirm the change in both your original and new email.

If you no longer have access to your old email, please [contact Support](mailto:support@syncplan.me).
