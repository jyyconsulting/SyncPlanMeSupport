---
title: "About SyncPlan.Me"
---

[SyncPlan.Me](http://SyncPlan.Me) was developed for people with multiple calendars to juggle.

The idea behind [SyncPlan.Me](http://SyncPlan.Me) is simple:

Connect your personal calendar, tell us about your work schedule, and we’ll take care of making sure the right appointments get sent to your work calendar.

We don't need access to your work calendar because the invites get sent to it from your personal calendar (we just need to know where to send it).

And with Stealth Mode, you can keep your personal appointment details private, while still blocking your time at work.
